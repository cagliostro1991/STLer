## STLer 0.1.0-beta.2

Second beta. The window works differently now: settings apply on their own, and it is easier to use with the keyboard and a screen reader. Please change a color or a view and check that the icons follow, and tell me what breaks.

### New
- Settings apply on their own a second after your last change. A newer change of the same type cancels the older job and starts over, and adding a folder to the scope starts rendering it at once.
- Regenerate draws every icon of a type again, also where it is up to date (button on the type page, and in the Icons menu). Clear still asks first.
- Menu bar: Check for Updates, an Icons menu (Regenerate, Clear, Show Journal with shortcuts) and a Help menu.
- A website, https://cagliostro1991.github.io/STLer/, and a wiki, https://github.com/cagliostro1991/STLer/wiki, linked from the app.

### Changed
- The Apply button and the floating Apply panel are gone.
- `stler follow` is called "background helper" in the window. Long explanations moved into "i" popovers, and the welcome sheet is shorter.
- Status text keeps its contrast (only the symbol is colored), a type that is off is marked "Off" in the sidebar, sliders show their value, Reduce Motion is respected, and icon-only buttons have labels for VoiceOver.
- US spelling "color" in the window.

### Fixed
- An empty row in the STEP engine section when OpenCASCADE is installed.

### Known issues
- The build is not notarized yet, see Install.
- A helper installed by beta 1 keeps running its old copy until you press Reinstall in Terminal on the General page.
- OBJ and PLY are not in this release.

### Install
1. Download `STLer-0.1.0-beta.2-macos.zip`, unpack it.
2. Move `STLer.app` to Applications. The build is not notarized yet: the first time, right-click the app and choose Open, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/STLer.app`.
3. Open the app, follow the welcome sheet to install `stler follow` (optional, it keeps icons up to date by itself).
