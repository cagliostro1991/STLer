# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) 2.1, in short form.

## Our pledge

Everyone should be able to take part, whatever their experience, background, language, age, body, identity or opinions, without being harassed.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are new to 3D printing, CAD or macOS internals.
- Assume good faith, and say it plainly when something is unclear instead of guessing the worst.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Accept feedback the same way.
- English is the working language of the project, but questions in other languages are welcome, a translation tool is fine.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, or sexualised language or images.
- Publishing someone's private information (addresses, e-mail, files they did not want shared).
- Spam, advertising unrelated to the project, and pressuring maintainers for fast answers. This is a spare-time project, replies can take days.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, the wiki and any other place where the project is represented.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete comments, close threads and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, write privately to the maintainer [@cagliostro1991](https://github.com/cagliostro1991) through GitHub (a [private security advisory](https://github.com/cagliostro1991/STLer/security/advisories/new) reaches only the maintainer, and works for conduct reports too). Reports are treated confidentially.
